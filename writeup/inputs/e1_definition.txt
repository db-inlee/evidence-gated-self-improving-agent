E1 definition (Ticket 007 section 2), fixed before any E1 value is computed
==========================================================================

Status: exploratory, descriptive. Not a pre-registered analysis and not a test.

Inputs (read only; no new runs, no re-analysis):
- results/ticket-005/analysis.json (sha256 51d51c9b01e5471e72c7927133d9f9c5732a46036d7867ba82359601e89fc982)
  candidates[*].oracle[m].delta and candidates[*].oracle[m].cells (the "exact" rational strings)
- results/ticket-006/analysis.json (sha256 1de4e7d3cab607a68c7e8c213e342a010ccf9d2956635b50e4fbd896d14eaa5f)
  candidates[*].oracle_l3[m].delta and candidates[*].oracle_l3[m].cells (the "exact" rational strings)
- m in {correct_disposition, evidence_safe}; every candidate in the frozen pool (12 proposer, 7 control).

Definition (exact rational arithmetic):
  E1 = (1/3)(Δ_bad,L2 + Δ_dep,L2 + Δ_traffic,L2)
  where Δ_f,L2 is the Ticket 005 oracle cell estimate for family f at level L2
  (cells "bad_deployment/L2", "dependency_failure/L2", "traffic_spike/L2").
Meaning: an approximation of the overall oracle effect estimate if L2-like incidents made up each
whole family, because an L3 incident combines the two L2 subtypes of its family.

Reported per candidate and metric (proposer 12 primary, control 7 auxiliary):
  Δ_L1/L2 (Ticket 005 overall), E1, Δ_L3 (Ticket 006 overall), residual Δ_L3 − E1,
  and per family Δ_f,L2 next to Δ_f,L3.

Summary values (fixed in advance; proposer, correct_disposition):
  1. the number of candidates with |Δ_L3 − E1| < |Δ_L3 − Δ_L1/L2|
  2. the mean absolute value of Δ_L3 − E1 and of Δ_L3 − Δ_L1/L2
No verdicts, intervals or thresholds are applied to E1.

Note on the rejected first draft: re-weighting the L1/L2 cells as (1/3) per family with the
L1 and L2 cells averaged inside a family equals the Ticket 005 overall Δ exactly (the six L1/L2
oracle cells have equal size, 50), so it was replaced by the definition above before computing.

Limitation sentence (paper):
  E1 is a post-hoc, exploratory approximation. The cell-weight explanation was formed after seeing
  the L3 results; only the definition of E1 was fixed before computing it. Each L2 cell mixes two
  distractor subtypes half and half, while every L3 incident contains both, so E1 ignores
  interactions between the two distractors. L2 cells hold 50 incidents and use different seeds
  from L3. E1 carries no uncertainty interval and is not a test.
